# Brick / fired clay

**Class:** mineral, ceramic. **Uses:** structural & veneer walls, paving, exposed interior feature walls.

## Physical property profile
- Acoustic absorption (§1): **low → reflective** (exposed brick is reverberant), though coursing scatters some.
- Reflectance & gloss (§2): matte; low glare.
- Fractal dimension (§3): **mid** — coursing + surface texture sit in a liked band.
- Thermal effusivity (§4): cool–neutral; high thermal mass.
- Hardness/roughness (§5): hard, **rough** — tactile "honest" texture.
- VOC (§6): inert, very low.
- Natural-material gestalt (§7): **moderate** — "earthy/traditional," warmer read than concrete.

## Semantic / cultural meaning
Tradition, craft, warmth, human scale, permanence; rarely negative. Regional/heritage associations strong.

## Caveats / thin spots
Like concrete/stone, brick's measured cost is **acoustic** (reverberation), fixable with absorption. Affective
warmth vs concrete is a framework claim resting on texture + gestalt — [RAG] the preference literature.
