# Cork (flooring, wall tiles, pinboard)

**Class:** natural, cellular (bark). **Uses:** flooring, wall covering, underlay, acoustic tiles.

## Physical property profile
- Acoustic absorption (§1): **moderate–high** — cellular structure absorbs; also cuts impact noise.
- Reflectance & gloss (§2): matte; low glare.
- Fractal dimension (§3): **mid**, natural granular figure.
- Thermal effusivity (§4): **low → feels warm/soft** underfoot.
- Hardness/roughness (§5): soft, resilient, low contact stress.
- VOC (§6): **low**; renewable/biodegradable (watch binders in composite tiles).
- Natural-material gestalt (§7): **moderate–strong** ("natural, eco").
