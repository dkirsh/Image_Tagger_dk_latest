# Natural stone (marble, granite, limestone; polished or honed)

**Class:** mineral, crystalline. **Uses:** floors, worktops, cladding, façades, feature walls.

## Physical property profile
- Acoustic absorption (§1): **very low → reflective**; long reverberation (like concrete).
- Reflectance & gloss (§2): **polished = high gloss/glare**; honed/matte = low glare. Property, not material.
- Fractal dimension (§3): low–mid (veining/grain can add mid-fractal figure).
- Thermal effusivity (§4): **high → feels cold**; high thermal mass.
- Hardness/roughness (§5): very hard, smooth-to-textured.
- VOC (§6): inert, very low emission.
- Natural-material gestalt (§7): **moderate** — reads as "natural/premium" but cold, not warm.

## Semantic / cultural meaning
Luxury, permanence, prestige, "natural quality"; also coldness and formality. Marble ≠ granite ≠ limestone in
cultural read — refine per stone at elaboration.

## Caveats / thin spots
Gloss (glare) and reverberation are the measured costs; both are finish/treatment-dependent, not intrinsic to
"stone." Radon is real but niche and location-specific — grade contested, don't generalise.
