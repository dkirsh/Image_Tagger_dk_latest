# Living plants / interior greenery

**Class:** living / biophilic. **Uses:** potted plants, green walls, planted atria, foliage screens.
*A material register in the encyclopedia because a planted surface acts on people through the same mediators
as a built one, plus air-quality and strong "nature" gestalt.*

## Physical property profile
- Acoustic absorption (§1): **moderate** — dense foliage/green walls scatter and absorb mid/high frequencies.
- Reflectance & gloss (§2): matte, diffuse; low glare.
- Fractal dimension (§3): **high, natural** — foliage sits in/above the preferred mid-fractal band.
- Thermal effusivity (§4): n/a as touch surface; adds humidity/evaporative cooling.
- Hardness/roughness (§5): soft, compliant.
- VOC (§6): **net-positive** — plants remove some VOCs/CO₂ (magnitude debated at room scale); low emission.
- Natural-material gestalt (§7): **strongest of any material** — it *is* nature, not a reference to it.

## Semantic / cultural meaning
Life, care, health, calm, status ("cared-for space"); rarely negative except maintenance/allergen concerns.

## Caveats / thin spots
Restoration/affect is the firmest result in the whole encyclopedia after acoustics. The **air-quality** claim
is real by mechanism but its **room-scale magnitude is contested** — grade honestly, don't oversell "plants
clean your air." Maintenance and allergen load are product/species-specific.
