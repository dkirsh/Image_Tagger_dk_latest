# Wood (solid timber / veneer)

**Class:** natural, cellulosic. **Uses:** flooring, wall cladding, ceilings, furniture, joinery.

## Physical property profile
- Acoustic absorption (§1): **low–moderate** as solid; thin panels/slatted-over-cavity raise it. Solid wood
  is fairly reflective — contributes reverberation unless perforated/backed.
- Reflectance & gloss (§2): **matte to satin**, mid reflectance; low glare (unless high-gloss lacquered).
- Fractal dimension (§3): grain figure sits in the **mid-fractal** band people prefer.
- Thermal effusivity (§4): **low → feels warm** to the touch.
- Hardness/roughness (§5): moderate; warm, "soft-hard" tactile.
- VOC (§6): solid wood **low**; engineered wood/adhesives (formaldehyde) can be **high** — depends on product,
  not "wood."
- Natural-material gestalt (§7): strong; reads as "nature."

## Impacts
| dimension | direction | via mediator | evidence | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| stress / autonomic | ↓ arousal, calming | §7 (matte+mid-fractal+warm+"nature") | framework→firm | Lipovac & Burnard 2021 |
| affect / mood | ↑ warmth, comfort, preference | §4, §7, §9 | framework→firm | wood-office RCT |
| cognitive performance | inconsistent / small | — | **contested (mixed)** | Lipovac & Burnard 2021 |
| preference / likeability | generally high; "warm, honest, quality" | §9 | framework | Karana MDD |
| health | good **if low-VOC solid**; engineered wood may off-gas | §6 | firm (IAQ) | COGfx |
| neurodiverse | usually low sensory load (matte, warm, predictable) | §8 | framework | ASPECTSS |

## Semantic / cultural meaning
Warmth, nature, craft, authenticity, quality; strong cross-cultural positive valence, though *species* and
*finish* carry finer meanings (oak=solid/traditional; bamboo=eco; high-gloss=luxury or "fake"). Beliefs about
proper use: welcomed in homes/healthcare/hospitality; sometimes read as "too domestic" for high-tech or civic
gravitas.

## Caveats / thin spots
The calming/affect result is solid; the **cognitive-performance** claim is where wood marketing overreaches —
grade it contested. "Wood" is not one material for health: solid low-VOC ≠ formaldehyde-bonded composite.
