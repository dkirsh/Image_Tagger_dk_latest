# Metal (steel, aluminium; sheet, mesh, polished or coated)

**Class:** metallic. **Uses:** structure, cladding, furniture, fixtures, ceilings, screens.

## Physical property profile
- Acoustic absorption (§1): **very low → reflective** solid; perforated metal over cavity can absorb.
- Reflectance & gloss (§2): **high/specular** unless matte-coated — a primary glare source.
- Fractal dimension (§3): ~0 (smooth) unless textured/perforated.
- Thermal effusivity (§4): **very high → feels cold**.
- Hardness/roughness (§5): very hard, smooth.
- VOC (§6): inert; coatings/paints vary.
- Natural-material gestalt (§7): absent — "engineered/industrial."

## Semantic / cultural meaning
Precision, modernity, strength, hygiene; also coldness, clinical/industrial institution. Finish drives the read.

## Caveats / thin spots
Two intrinsic costs (glare §2, cold touch §4) plus reverberation §1 — all finish/treatment-modifiable. No
strong positive wellbeing channel; value is functional/aesthetic. [RAG] the preference/semantic cells.
