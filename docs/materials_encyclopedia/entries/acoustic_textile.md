# Acoustic textile / porous absorber (felt panels, fabric-wrapped, carpet, curtains)

**Class:** textile / porous. **Uses:** wall & ceiling panels, baffles, carpet, drapery, upholstery.

## Physical property profile
- Acoustic absorption (§1): **high** (the defining property) — high α across mid/high frequencies; the
  primary lever for shortening reverberation and raising STI.
- Reflectance & gloss (§2): matte, low glare; diffuse.
- Fractal dimension (§3): soft, often mid-fractal weave/texture.
- Thermal effusivity (§4): **low → warm/soft** to the touch.
- Hardness/roughness (§5): soft; low-contact-stress.
- VOC (§6): **variable** — some synthetic textiles/adhesives/flame-retardants emit; specify low-emission.
- Natural-material gestalt (§7): moderate (esp. wool/felt).

## Semantic / cultural meaning
Softness, comfort, quiet, care, domesticity; carpet also reads "warm/homely" or "dated/unhygienic" depending
on culture and setting. Beliefs about proper use: welcomed where calm and speech clarity matter (classrooms,
clinics, offices, homes); resisted in wet/clinical zones for hygiene.

## Caveats / thin spots
This is the material whose primary impact is the **firmest** in the whole encyclopedia (acoustics→cognition).
The main trade-offs are IAQ (VOCs/flame-retardants) and, for carpet, allergen/dust capture — both
product-specific, not intrinsic.
